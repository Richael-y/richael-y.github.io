About

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Singapore Management University, advised by Prof. Xiaofei Xie. My research focuses on automated penetration testing, black-box web security testing, and LLM agents.

My research builds on hands-on experience in CTF competitions, attack-and-defense exercises, and real-world penetration testing.
